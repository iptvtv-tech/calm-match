Cookie policy

Last updated:

No tracking cookies. Calm Match uses no advertising, analytics or social media cookies. It only stores what is strictly necessary for the site to work, so there is nothing to opt in to. The notice you saw on your first visit is just to let you know.

Third parties

There are no third-party trackers on this site. If you follow the donate link, the donation platform may set its own cookies on its own website; see its cookie policy.

Managing storage

You can delete everything Calm Match stores on a device from the Grown-ups area (Children → Delete everything on this device), or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Blocking storage completely will stop settings and progress being saved between visits, but the games will still run.
