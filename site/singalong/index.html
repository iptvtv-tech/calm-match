Sing along

Pick a song. The words light up as the tune plays, so your child can sing along at their own pace.

    Children's songs and nursery rhymes in English and Irish (Gaeilge), such as Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Old MacDonald Had a Farm, Incy Wincy Spider and Happy Birthday. Each song has Irish words written to the same tune. Free, with no ads and no microphone, and it works offline.

    Words in Irish and English: Twinkle Twinkle Little Star · Baa, Baa, Black Sheep · Mary Had a Little Lamb · Row, Row, Row Your Boat · Are You Sleeping? · Hot Cross Buns · Rain, Rain, Go Away · Happy Birthday · London Bridge Is Falling Down · Old MacDonald Had a Farm · This Old Man · Incy Wincy Spider · Hickory Dickory Dock · Jack and Jill · Yankee Doodle · Jingle Bells · Humpty Dumpty · Ring a Ring o' Roses · Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes · Bingo · Here We Go Round the Mulberry Bush · Hush, Little Baby · Lavender's Blue
