Calm Match — Gaeilge check

Go raibh maith agat. Calm Match is a free site of calm learning games for neurodivergent children, about 3 to 9, used with a parent or teacher. The Irish was written by the site's maker, who is not fluent, and is read aloud to children, so natural spoken Irish matters more than literal translation. Short is good: these show on buttons and cards. For each line, tap OK if it's fine, or type a correction (or a question) in the box. Words in braces like {name} or {n} are filled in by the site, so please say if the words around them need to change (a number before a noun, for example). Everything you tick or type is saved as you go, with your email, so we know who to thank.
