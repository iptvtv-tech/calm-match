Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Previous versions

The short version

  • Without an account, nothing you or your child do in the games leaves your device.
  • With an optional parent account, we store your email address and, for each child, an avatar emoji and their game settings. Skill progress is stored only if you switch that on.
  • We never collect a child's name, age, photo, voice, location or any health or diagnosis information. There is nowhere to enter it. Photos and recordings a grown-up adds stay on this device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share data for marketing.
  • Reading with a child? Try our privacy page for children.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time from the Account page. For anything else about your data, email privacy@calmmatch.com.

Who we are

Calm Match is run by J.P Cunningham, who is the data controller for the information described here.

  • Questions about your data or this policy: privacy@calmmatch.com
  • Everything else: hello@calmmatch.com

Using Calm Match without an account

The games run entirely in your browser. The following is saved in your browser's local storage on your device only, and is never sent to us:

  • Each child's nickname and avatar, as you enter them.
  • Game and sensory settings, such as theme, sound, read-aloud and contrast.
  • Progress: skill scores, recent session results, stickers.
  • Photos you add in photo mode. They are shrunk on the device and never uploaded, even if you have an account.
  • Things you make for your child: schedules, the Talk board, stories and how-tos, "My pictures" with the words you type, the token board's reward, and any voice recordings or short videos you add. These stay on the device and are never sent to us, even if you have an account.

Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voice. Sound is generated on the device. We never use location. The microphone is used only when a grown-up presses Record to record a word or a story page, and only on this device; Calm Match never listens to children. Calm Match doesn't switch on the camera itself: if you add a photo or video, you choose it, or take it with your device's own camera app.

The Talk board doesn't save or send anything a child taps.

Backup files. If you choose "Save backup file", your browser saves a file with everything above, including photos and recordings, wherever you choose. We never see it. Keep it somewhere private, and delete it when you no longer need it.

You can delete this data at any time: Grown-ups area → Children → Delete everything on this device, or by clearing site data in your browser.

What we never collect

We do not collect children's names, ages, dates of birth, photos, voice recordings, location, or any information about health, diagnosis or disability. Please don't type any of these into nickname fields. If you contact us by email, please don't include them either.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase stores account data. Our project is hosted in the EU (Ireland, eu-west-1) region. Supabase acts as our processor under a data processing agreement.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends account emails for us: confirming your address, sign-in links, password resets, and the confirmation when you delete your account. It receives your email address and the email content only. Brevo counts how many emails are opened and links clicked; we have set this to anonymous, so it isn't linked to you.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and delivers it to your browser. Like any web host it processes IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against attacks.
  • Emails you send to hello@calmmatch.com or privacy@calmmatch.com are forwarded by Cloudflare to our Google (Gmail) inbox, where they are kept for up to 12 months, or 2 years after a data request is closed.
  • If you choose to donate, Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland) takes the payment on its own page, under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. We receive your email address, the amount, and your name if your payment method shares it, and keep these for 6 years for tax records.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account, or until it has not been used for 24 months (we email a warning 30 days before). When you delete it, we send one confirmation email to the address on the account and then keep nothing.
  • Progress backup: until you switch it off, remove the child, or delete your account.
  • Hosting security logs: kept for short periods set by our providers.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do yourself on the Account page: download your data, switch progress backup off, change your email, or delete your account. For anything else, see Making a data request below.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (Ireland) at www.dataprotection.ie, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Making a data request

Email privacy@calmmatch.com for any request about your data, including:

  • a copy of the data we hold about you (a "subject access request")
  • correcting or deleting your data, including deleting your account if you can't sign in
  • restricting or objecting to how we use your data
  • questions or concerns about this policy or how we handle data

What to include: what you would like us to do, and the email address of your account, if you have one. Please don't send ID documents, and please don't include your child's name, age or any health information.

Checking it's you: if you have an account, we may reply to the account's email address to confirm the request before acting on it. We never ask for passwords.

How long it takes: we reply within one month. If a request is complex we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you within the first month if so. Requests are free.

Data on your device: games, nicknames, progress and photos stored on your device never reach us, so we can't see or delete them. You can delete them yourself: Grown-ups area → Children → Delete everything on this device.

Children

Calm Match is designed to be used by children with a parent, carer or teacher who sets it up. Children cannot create accounts, and the games ask a grown-up question before anyone can open the settings. Any account data is provided and controlled by the adult.

There is a version of this policy for children, in simple words with pictures, to read together.

Children under 13 (including the US COPPA rule)

Calm Match is made for children, so we follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as well as GDPR.

  • We do not collect personal information from children. Children never create accounts, and nothing a child types, taps or plays is sent to us. Nicknames, progress and photos stay in the browser on your device.
  • No chat, no sharing, no ads, no tracking. There is nothing a child can post or send to anyone, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
  • Technical identifiers. Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) sees the device's IP address when a page loads. It is used only to deliver the site and protect it from attacks, and never to build a profile, advertise or contact anyone. If a parent has signed in on the device, a sign-in token is used only to sync the parent's chosen settings.
  • Read-aloud uses only voices built into the device, so spoken words are not sent to any online voice service.
  • Parent accounts are for adults only. The information in them comes from, and is controlled by, the parent. A parent can see, download or delete it at any time on the Account page.
  • If a child contacts us by email, we use the address only to reply, ideally to a parent, and then delete the message.
  • If we learn we have received personal information from a child under 13 without a parent's consent, we delete it. Parents can contact privacy@calmmatch.com at any time.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Account data is protected by row-level security, so each account can only ever read its own records. Passwords are hashed and never visible to us.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how account data is used, we will tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
