Using Calm Match without an account

The games run entirely in your browser. The following is saved in your browser's local storage on your device only, and is never sent to us:

  • Each child's nickname and avatar, as you enter them.
  • Game and sensory settings, such as theme, sound, read-aloud and contrast.
  • Progress: skill scores, recent session results, stickers.
  • Photos you add in photo mode. They are shrunk on the device and never uploaded, even if you have an account.

Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voice. Sound is generated on the device. We do not use the camera, microphone or location. If you add photos, you choose them from your device yourself.

You can delete this data at any time: Grown-ups area → Children → Delete everything on this device, or by clearing site data in your browser.

What we never collect

We do not collect children's names, ages, dates of birth, photos, voice recordings, location, or any information about health, diagnosis or disability. Please don't type any of these into nickname fields. If you contact us by email, please don't include them either.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase stores account data. Our project is hosted in the region. Supabase acts as our processor under a data processing agreement.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends account emails for us: confirming your address, sign-in links, password resets, and the confirmation when you delete your account. It receives your email address and the email content only.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and delivers it to your browser. Like any web host it processes IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against attacks.
  • If accounts are switched on and the site can't load its own copy of the sign-in library, your browser fetches it from jsDelivr, a public code CDN, which sees your IP address.
  • If you choose to donate, the donation platform processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only what that platform shares with creators, typically your name or display name, email and the amount.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account. When you delete it, we send one confirmation email to the address on the account and then keep nothing. We delete accounts with no sign-in for 24 months, after emailing a warning first.
  • Progress backup: until you switch it off, remove the child, or delete your account.
  • Hosting security logs: kept for short periods set by our providers.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do yourself on the Account page: download your data, switch progress backup off, change your email, or delete your account. For anything else, see Making a data request below.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (Ireland) at www.dataprotection.ie, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Making a data request

Email for any request about your data, including:

  • a copy of the data we hold about you (a "subject access request")
  • correcting or deleting your data, including deleting your account if you can't sign in
  • restricting or objecting to how we use your data
  • questions or concerns about this policy or how we handle data

What to include: what you would like us to do, and the email address of your account, if you have one. Please don't send ID documents, and please don't include your child's name, age or any health information.

Checking it's you: if you have an account, we may reply to the account's email address to confirm the request before acting on it. We never ask for passwords.

How long it takes: we reply within one month. If a request is complex we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you within the first month if so. Requests are free.

Data on your device: games, nicknames, progress and photos stored on your device never reach us, so we can't see or delete them. You can delete them yourself: Grown-ups area → Children → Delete everything on this device.

Children

Calm Match is designed to be used by children with a parent, carer or teacher who sets it up. Children cannot create accounts, and the games ask a grown-up question before anyone can open the settings. Any account data is provided and controlled by the adult.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Account data is protected by row-level security, so each account can only ever read its own records. Passwords are hashed and never visible to us.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how account data is used, we will tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
