Support the site

Calm Match is free, has no ads and never sells data. If it helps your family or your class, you can chip in towards running costs.

Donations aren't set up yet.

Where the money goes

  • Web hosting and the account database.
  • A domain name.
  • Time to build new games and themes.

Good to know

  • Donating is completely optional. Every feature is free, and always will be for everyone.
  • The donate button only appears on grown-up pages, never inside the games.
  • Payments are handled by the donation platform. We never see your card details.
  • Calm Match is not a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible.
