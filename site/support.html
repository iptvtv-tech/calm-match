Help keep Calm Match free

No ads, no tracking, no paid extras, and it stays that way. If the games help your child, your family or your class, you can chip in towards the running costs.

Donations open soon. Until then, the best help is telling other families about Calm Match.

What it costs to run

Calm Match is built and run by one person, J.P Cunningham, in Ireland. These are the ongoing costs donations help with.

Hosting and accounts

Keeping the games fast and online, and the database for optional parent accounts, hosted in the EU.

Domain and email

The calmmatch.com address, plus email for questions from parents and the sign-in emails for accounts.

New games and themes

Time to design, build and test new games, picture themes and settings, and to fix anything that doesn't work well.

Irish and other languages

Getting translations checked by fluent speakers, so every child can play in the language they know best.

Our promises, donation or not

  • Every feature stays free for everyone. Donating never unlocks anything.
  • No ads, ever, and no tracking or analytics cookies.
  • Children never see a donate button. It only appears on pages for grown-ups.
  • We never sell or share data. Children's names and photos never leave your device.

Free ways to help

Money isn't the only thing that keeps Calm Match going. These help just as much.

Tell another family

Share Calm Match in a parents' group, with your child's school, or with your OT, speech therapist or SNA.

Suggest ideas

Which themes does your child love? Which games or settings would help? Most new features start with a parent's idea.

Report a problem

Something confusing, a picture that looks wrong on your device, or a setting that didn't help? Tell us and we'll fix it.

Check our Irish

Are you a fluent Irish speaker, or a Gaelscoil teacher? Help us make sure the games sound natural to children.

For all of these, email hello@calmmatch.com. We read every message.

Questions

Is Calm Match a charity?

No. It is run by an individual, so donations are gifts towards running costs and are not tax-deductible.

Do I get anything extra if I donate?

No. Every feature is free for everyone, and always will be. A donation simply helps keep it that way.

Can a school, therapy practice or organisation donate?

Yes, and thank you. If you need a receipt or invoice, email hello@calmmatch.com and we'll sort it out.

Who handles the payment?

Stripe, a regulated payment company. You pay on Stripe's own secure page, and we never see your card details. Stripe emails you a receipt.

Can I get a refund?

Donations are generally not refundable, but if you donated by mistake, email us within 14 days and we'll do our best to help.
