Accessibility statement

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Calm Match is built for children who may experience the world differently, so accessibility is part of the design rather than an add-on. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What's built in

  • Sensory settings for each child: sound on or off, read-aloud with three speeds, no motion, high contrast and bigger buttons.
  • Respects your device's "reduce motion" and dark mode settings.
  • Predictable layouts: a picture schedule, the same screen shape for every turn, and no time limits.
  • No flashing content, no sudden sounds, and no sound for wrong answers.
  • Number-key answers (1, 2, 3…) for keyboard and switch users, with visible focus outlines.
  • Every picture has a text label for screen readers.
  • Large tap targets, plus a larger option.
  • Works offline after the first visit.
  • Games in English or Irish (Gaeilge). Read-aloud in Irish needs an Irish voice on the device; without one, read-aloud stays silent rather than mispronounce words.
  • Optional errorless start, three celebration styles including "very calm", and a choice of sticker sets or none.
  • Photo mode, so children can practise with pictures of their own things.
  • Visual schedules, a First/Then board, a token board and picture stories, with symbols as well as words, and optional recordings in a familiar voice.
  • A simple Talk board with fixed button positions, and a printable paper copy.

Known limits

  • Pictures use your device's emoji, so they look a little different on Apple, Android and Windows devices.
  • Read-aloud depends on the voices installed on the device and may not be available in every browser.
  • "What's missing?" shows the pictures for a few seconds before one is hidden (an extra second in no-motion mode). Switch the game off in Settings if this is hard.
  • "Trace a letter" needs a finger, mouse or stylus. With number keys on, a Done button lets keyboard and switch users move on; it counts as practice, not as learned.
  • The "press and hold" grown-ups button is hard for some people to use. Keyboard users get a sum question instead, and you can switch everyone to the sum question in Settings.

Feedback

If something doesn't work for you or your child, please tell us at hello@calmmatch.com. We aim to reply within 10 working days.
