For grown-ups: this is our privacy policy in simple words, for children aged about 5 to 11. Read it together, one card at a time. The full version is our privacy policy.

Your games are yours

Here is how Calm Match keeps your things private. Private means only you and your grown-up can see them.

  1. Your games stay here

    The games you play and the stickers you win stay on this tablet or computer. We don't take them away.

  2. We don't ask your name

    We never ask for your real name, how old you are, or where you live. Your grown-up can give you a nickname and a picture, like a dinosaur or a star.

  3. No camera, no listening

    Calm Match never takes pictures of you, never listens to you, and never finds out where you are. If your grown-up adds family photos, or records their voice for your words and stories, those stay on this tablet too.

  4. No adverts, nobody watching

    There are no adverts and no one is watching what you tap. Nobody can buy anything about you, ever.

  5. Your grown-up can save settings

    If your grown-up makes an account, we keep your picture and your game settings safe, so the games look the same on another tablet. If they choose, we also keep how you're getting on in the games. We still never keep your name.

  6. You can wipe it all away

    If you want everything gone, ask your grown-up. They can delete it all with one button, and it's gone for good.

  7. If something feels wrong

    If anything on a screen makes you worried, stop and tell a grown-up you trust. You won't be in trouble.

For grown-ups

  • Calm Match is designed to be set up and used with an adult. Children can't make accounts, and settings sit behind a grown-up question.
