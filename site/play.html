    Rules in use

    Inside a game: 3 right first time in a row adds an answer choice. 2 misses on one turn puts a dashed outline on the right answer and removes a choice next turn. In Pairs, two good boards shorten the peek at the start. Between sessions: a game where fewer than half the turns were right first time is flagged, and next time the engine practises the skill before it. Sessions start with the strongest game and end on something familiar. Once a session starts its plan never changes.
