      How the engine decides

      Inside a game: 3 right first time in a row adds an answer choice. 2 misses on one turn puts a dashed outline on the right answer and removes a choice next turn. In Pairs, two good boards shorten the peek at the start. With errorless start on, a new skill begins with the right answer highlighted; after 2 right answers the help drops to an outline, then to no help. Helped answers don't count towards "how sure". Between sessions: a game where fewer than half the independent turns were right first time is flagged, and next time the engine practises the skill before it. Sessions start with the strongest game and end on something familiar. Once a session starts its plan never changes.

      Home screen

      Picture doors your child sees under the Start button. Turn off any your child doesn't need. With all of them off, the home screen shows only Start. Each child has their own choice.

      Token board

      Your child earns a token for each game finished and each schedule step done. When the board is full, it shows the reward you picked. Tokens are never taken away.

      Breaks

      The Break button offers breathing, bubbles, music or quiet. A break timer gives a 1-minute warning, then "Break time is over". Your child can always come back sooner.

      What's new — October 2026

      • Picture doors. Under Start, your child now has big picture doors to Sing along, the Alphabet book, Stories, Stickers, the Calm corner and My day. Turn any of them off in Settings › Home screen.

      Who is playing

      Nickname

      Emma, the voice that reads the games aloud, can't say names. Record your child's name in your own voice and Emma will use it, for example "Great work, Sam". Until then she leaves the name out. The recording stays on this device.

      Add a child

      Use a nickname only. Please don't enter a full name, age, diagnosis or anything medical. Nicknames stay on this device and are never uploaded.
