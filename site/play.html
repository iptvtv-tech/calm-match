      How the engine decides

      Inside a game: 3 right first time in a row adds an answer choice. 2 misses on one turn puts a dashed outline on the right answer and removes a choice next turn. In Pairs, two good boards shorten the peek at the start. With errorless start on, a new skill begins with the right answer highlighted; after 2 right answers the help drops to an outline, then to no help. Helped answers don't count towards "how sure". Between sessions: a game where fewer than half the independent turns were right first time is flagged, and next time the engine practises the skill before it. Sessions start with the strongest game and end on something familiar. Once a session starts its plan never changes.
