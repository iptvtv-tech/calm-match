Credits

Symbols

Most symbols on the Talk board, in schedules and in stories are from the Mulberry Symbol set by Steve Lee, used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence. Calm Match uses a selection of them, with spacing and fixed sizes removed so they load faster and fit their buttons. They are not otherwise changed.

A few symbols Mulberry doesn't draw (for example "you", "not", "stop", "yes" and "no") were made for Calm Match. They are shared under the same licence, so anyone can reuse them.

Core words

The 36 words on the Talk board's Words page are the Universal Core vocabulary from Project Core at the Center for Literacy and Disability Studies, University of North Carolina. Calm Match isn't connected with Project Core.

Pictures

Game pictures are emoji drawn by your own device, so they look different on different phones and tablets.

Stories

The starter stories in "My stories" were written for Calm Match. Social Stories™ is a trademark of Carol Gray. Calm Match's "My stories" feature isn't connected with or endorsed by Carol Gray.
