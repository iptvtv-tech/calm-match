For schools and classrooms

Turn on classroom mode in the Grown-ups area, under Children and device. Each child taps their own picture to start, and the tablet goes back to "Who is playing?" when they finish. Every child keeps their own settings and progress, all on that tablet.

A printable summary and a spreadsheet of sessions can be shared with parents or a therapist, and a backup file moves a child's set-up between home and school.

How it adapts

Calm Match uses simple, visible rules rather than a black box. You can read every decision in the Grown-ups area.

  • Inside a game: three right answers in a row adds a choice. Two misses on one turn shows a dashed outline on the right answer and takes a choice away.
  • Skill map: 42 small skills across 16 games. New skills open as earlier ones are learned, for example Pattern A B before Pattern A A B.
  • Errorless start (optional): new skills begin with the right answer highlighted, and the help fades as your child gets them right.
  • Session plan: each session starts with the strongest game, practises something that is ready to learn, and ends on something familiar. If a skill was hard last time, it steps back to the one before it.

Parents see a skill map, a plain-language summary, a history of sessions, and the reason behind each choice the engine makes.

Private by default

Everything works without an account, and then nothing leaves the device. If you make an optional parent account, we store your email plus each child's avatar and settings. Children's names, photos and recordings stay on the device. There is no place to enter a diagnosis, and we never ask for one. Read the privacy policy.

What it is not

Calm Match is a set of practice games. It is not a medical device, a diagnostic tool, or a replacement for therapy, teaching or professional advice. It works best with a grown-up nearby.

Keeping it free

Calm Match has no ads and no paid features. If it helps your family, you can chip in towards hosting costs. It's entirely optional.
